(top-right menu, after signing in) to set up real accounts for yourself,
your dad, the accountant, and the inventory manager.

Installing on a phone (so no one needs this link again):
  Android/Chrome — open the link once, a banner appears with an
    "Install Karein" button. Tap it, done — icon appears on the home screen.
  iPhone/Safari — open the link, tap the Share icon, choose
    "Add to Home Screen". Safari doesn't support an automatic install
    button, so this manual step is the only way on iOS.
  After installing, it opens straight from the home screen icon like
  any other app — this link is only needed the first time.
