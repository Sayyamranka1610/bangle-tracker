HOW TO USE
  1. Open gemini.google.com (Google AI Pro account)
  2. Upload your bangle photo
  3. Copy ONE full prompt below and paste it
  4. Generate, CHECK THE SPELLING of all text + the plating colour,
     regenerate if any word is wrong or the colour shifted
  5. Add your logo yourself afterward

WHAT CHANGED IN v2
  - PLATING FIX: prompts keep your EXACT plating (rose gold stays rose gold).
  - No "gold glow" language that warm-shifted rose gold to yellow.

FORMAT
  - 16:9 wide landscape · bangles one side, headline + tagline the other
  - Small quality-badge row (tiny icon + small label) along the bottom
  - NO logo (you add it yourself)

NOTE ON TEXT
  AI can misspell text. The headline (large) is reliable; the tiny quality
  labels are the most error-prone — always check before using.
