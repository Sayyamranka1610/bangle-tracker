HOW TO USE
  1. Open gemini.google.com (signed in with your Google AI Pro account)
  2. Upload your bangle photo
  3. Copy ONE full prompt block below and paste it
  4. Generate; check the design + colour, regenerate if anything shifted
  5. Repeat with the same photo for each style you want

WHAT CHANGED IN v3 (from v2)
  - GROUNDS: Royal Blue → Emerald Green;  Noir Charcoal → Deep Plum/Aubergine.
    Four grounds are now clearly distinct: Maroon (red), Emerald (green),
    Plum (purple), Champagne (light neutral).
  - PROPS: 3 of 4 placements are styled with tasteful, VARIED jewellery props
    (florals, petals, pearls, silk dupatta, bridal potli, velvet jewellery box,
    gold hair-vine, softly-blurred matching earrings / maang-tikka).
    NO necklaces. NO dark-red or dried roses (blush / ivory / white / peach only).
    P2 in every ground is kept clean and minimal (no props).
  - HERO: props are forced small, secondary and softly out of focus so the
    bangle design stays the largest, sharpest, most eye-catching element.
  - SHINE: metal must look bright, luminous and polished — never dull or muddy;
    the background colour must not tint or cast onto the metal.
  - CLEAN: any numbers, text, tags or watermarks in the source are removed.
  - POSE: bangles always stand upright / propped / tilted — never laid flat.

SYSTEM
  4 grounds  ×  4 placements  =  16 master styles
  Aspect ratio: 16:9 wide landscape (locked)
