HOW TO USE
  1. Open gemini.google.com (signed in with your Google AI Pro account)
  2. Upload your bangle photo
  3. Copy ONE full prompt block below and paste it
  4. Generate; check the design + colour, regenerate if anything shifted
  5. Repeat with the same photo for each style you want

WHAT CHANGED IN v2
  - PLATING FIX: prompts no longer say "gold" — they order the AI to keep
    your EXACT plating (rose gold stays rose gold, two-tone stays two-tone).
  - ANTI-DISTORTION: added "closed and intact, same shape" so the AI can't
    re-pose or warp the bangle; cushion shots now STAND propped (not re-angled).
  - Lighting says "gleam without changing colour" instead of "make gold glow".

SYSTEM
  4 grounds  ×  4 placements  =  16 master styles
  Cushion used in P3 + P4  (8 images)  ·  No cushion in P1 + P2  (8 images)
  Aspect ratio: 16:9 wide landscape (locked)
